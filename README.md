### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm **Michael Lin (林彥文)**, based in **Taichung, Taiwan** — Chief Strategy Officer at
[**AIWin Technology (慧穩科技)**](https://aiwin.com.tw/), where we build **WinHub.AI**,
a fusion AI SaaS platform that gets AI out of the notebook and onto the factory floor.

- 🎯 I work at the seam between **product strategy, MLOps platforms, and enterprise AI delivery**
- 🏭 AIWin has delivered **150+ AI projects across 30+ industries** — semiconductor, smart manufacturing, water resources, smart cities
- 🎤 **100+ enterprise talks & workshops** — helping teams go from "AI sounds nice" to a system in production
- 🧑‍🏫 Course design & instructor: AI literacy, no-code AI, and practical deployment
- 🌱 Still shipping code — mostly Python, Node/Vue, and LLM-powered internal tooling
- 🎓 M.A. in Risk Management & Insurance, **Feng Chia University**

> **Why "no-code"?** Most AI projects don't fail on the model — they fail on the handoff.
> So we build platforms where the person who owns the problem can also train, deploy, and trust the model.

### 🧩 What I build at AIWin

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AIWinOps** | Patent-grade no-code **vision AI** training platform — five image-analysis modes, 95–98% defect detection |
| **NumOps** | **Data AI + expert system** training platform with AutoML — 97.5% anomaly detection, ~60× faster than manual review |
| **WinEdge** | **Edge AI inference** system — deployment management, dashboards, multi-device fleet control |
| **WinChat** | **Enterprise knowledge hub** built on LLM + RAG, extensible into AI agents |

All four sit on **WinHub.AI**, AIWin's unified platform brand.
